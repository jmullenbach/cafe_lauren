## Task: write out a meal idea as a full recipe

The household picked this meal idea for a night this week: `title`, `description`, `method`, `total_min` and `cost_usd`. `main_ingredients` are the main items the idea was built around (with `have` = already in the pantry, `sale` = on sale this week); `why` is why it was suggested. Write the complete recipe in the household format.

- Keep the title, method and spirit of the idea. Use the main ingredients; prefer what is in `pantry` and on sale in `deals`.
- Serve `household_size` (with leftovers if the meal suits it). Every meal has a protein and a large vegetable portion or side.
- Full ingredient list with quantities; steps grouped under descriptive headings, one action each, every amount in **bold** inline.
- Honest prep/cook/total minutes, healthiness and deliciousness ratings, a cost estimate for the household, and leftover ideas ("Day 2: ...").
