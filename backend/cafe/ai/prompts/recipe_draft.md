## Task: write a recipe for the recipe box

Turn what the person gave you into one complete recipe in the household format: a description they typed, text they pasted, the text of a web page, or a photo of a recipe or a dish.

- Keep the source's ingredients and method when there is a source; scale to serve 5 (with leftovers if the meal suits it). Fill gaps sensibly.
- Prefer the household's methods (Sheet pan, Instant Pot, Le Creuset, Skillet) when the source allows.
- Every step's amounts in **bold** inline, steps grouped under descriptive headings, a one-sentence description, honest healthiness and deliciousness ratings, a cost estimate for 5, and leftover ideas ("Day 2: ...").
- If a web page holds several recipes or a lot of other text, use the main recipe only.
