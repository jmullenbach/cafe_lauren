## Task: suggest a replacement after a rejection

A person turned down `rejected` for `day`. Their reasons are in `reasons` and `reject_note`. Suggest one different meal for that night (kind `cook`) that answers those reasons. Do not suggest the rejected meal, or anything close to it. Start `why` with what they said, e.g. "You said: Too much work", then 1-2 more reasons. Prefer the recipe box; invent a full `new_recipe` only if nothing fits. Give `ingredients` flags.
