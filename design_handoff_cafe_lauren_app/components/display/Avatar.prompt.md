Household member marker — who requested, voted or is cooking. AvatarStack (same file) overlaps several.
```jsx
<Avatar name="Lauren" color="sage" />
<AvatarStack people={[{name:'Lauren',color:'sage'},{name:'Joe',color:'slate'},{name:'Leidy',color:'terra'}]} />
```
- Default household mapping: Lauren sage, Joe slate, Leidy terra.
