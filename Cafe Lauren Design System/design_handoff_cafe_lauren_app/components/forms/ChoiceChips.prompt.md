Tap-to-pick chips — the fastest way to give Café structured feedback.
```jsx
<ChoiceChips value={why} onChange={setWhy} options={['Too much work','Had it recently','Kids won\'t eat it','Too pricey']} />
<ChoiceChips multi={false} size="s" value={f} onChange={setF} options={['All','5 stars','Instant Pot','Quick']} />
```
- Selected = charcoal fill. Always pair reasons with an optional free-text Input.
